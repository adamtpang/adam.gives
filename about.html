About

Adam builds small, finished things.

adam.gives is the personal storefront of Adam Pang, also known as Adam Pangelinan. The site is operated by Anchor Marianas LLC and brings Adam's build services, paid products, and free tools into one plain menu.

Who Adam is

Adam is from Guam and currently lives in Malaysia. He works as an independent builder and has shipped dozens of live products, mostly solo with AI-assisted development. The public studio lists the products and opens each real destination, while Adam's personal site contains a broader account of his work.

This is a small operation, not a large agency. The menu is designed to make that legible: the buyer can see the specific outcome, the complete price, the expected delivery window, and the next action before starting a conversation.

What this site offers

The main service is a fixed-scope build sprint for a founder, small business, or funded team with one specific unbuilt thing. The sprint is scoped the same day it is requested, costs $1,500, and aims to ship one working outcome in about seven days. The buyer owns the code, repository, and deployment.

Smaller problems can go through a $199 power hour, where Adam and the buyer build live together. A completed sprint can continue on a $750 monthly plan for fixes, updates, and one new improvement each month. Separate paid products and free tools are listed clearly rather than blended into those services.

How the work is run

The operating principle is simple: state the whole price, keep the scope concrete, and do not make proof sound bigger than it is. The repository for this site is public, the policies name the operating company, and the contact page provides direct sales, booking, privacy, and legal routes.
