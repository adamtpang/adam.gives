What to include

For a power hour, describe the point where you are stuck and what a useful working result would look like at the end of the call. For a build sprint, describe the single outcome you want live, who will use it, and any existing code, system, or deadline that shapes the scope.

For a continuation request, name the sprint that already shipped and the next fix or improvement it needs. If a checkout link, page, or delivered product is not working, include the URL and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords, full payment card details, or other secrets by email.

Payments, privacy, and support

Stripe processes payments, so Adam and Anchor Marianas LLC do not receive or store full payment card details. Questions about personal information, deletion, correction, or the site's policies should go to the operator email above. The privacy policy explains what information is collected when someone emails, books a call, or purchases an offer.

If you are ready to compare scope and price before contacting Adam, the plain menu lists the service rungs, guarantees, and direct next actions in one page.
