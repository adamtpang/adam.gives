Privacy policy

Last updated 13 July 2026

Who we are

This website, adam.gives, and the offers listed on it are operated by Anchor Marianas LLC ("we", "us", or "our"), a limited liability company founded by Adam Pang in the United States. This policy explains what personal information we collect and how we use it. You can reach us at adam@anchormarianas.com.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you share when you email us or book a call, such as your name, email address, and anything you write to us.
  • Order and billing details when you buy an offer, handled through our payment processor. We receive a record of the purchase, not your full card number.
  • Project material you send us so we can do the work you hired us for.
  • Basic technical data, such as your browser type and general location, that our hosting provider records to keep the site running and secure.

How we use your information

  • To deliver the gifts and paid offers you request.
  • To process payments and keep accurate business records.
  • To reply to your messages and support your projects.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the site.
  • To meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. When you pay, your card details go directly to Stripe and are handled under Stripe's own privacy policy. We do not see or store your full payment card information.

Third party services

We rely on a small set of trusted providers to run this business, including Stripe for payments, Vercel for hosting, Cal.com for scheduling calls, and an email provider for correspondence. Each processes data only as needed to provide its service, under its own privacy terms.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies of its own. Our hosting provider may use essential cookies or logs to deliver and protect the site. Third party pages you open from our links, such as Stripe checkout or a booking page, may set their own cookies under their own policies.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers listed above so they can perform their function, or when required by law, or to protect our rights. If the business is ever transferred, information may pass to the new owner under this policy.

Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal and accounting requirements, then delete or anonymize it.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to stop contacting you. To make a request, email adam@anchormarianas.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

Children

The site is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

International visitors

We operate from the United States. If you visit from another country, your information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version on this page, with its last updated date, is the current one.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to adam@anchormarianas.com.
